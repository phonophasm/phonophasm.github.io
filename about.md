# About

<div class="card">
  <p><strong>Hi — I’m Pete (Phonophasm).</strong></p>

  <p>
    I design and perform immersive audio-visual experiences, VR instruments, and interactive installations —
    combining sound, interaction, and real-time visuals to create spaces for focus, reflection, and playful exploration.
  </p>

  <p>
    My background blends engineering and music with an MA in creative/interaction design. Recent work includes VR instrument
    prototypes, sensory-driven experiments, and tools that treat attention as something to care for, not compete for.
  </p>

  <h2>How I work</h2>
  <p>
    I’m drawn to participatory approaches and collaborative making — especially projects that prioritise listening,
    accessibility, and thoughtful pacing over spectacle. I care about how emerging technologies represent people, whose
    voices get amplified, and how we can design experiences that feel inclusive and human.
  </p>

  <h2>Life outside the studio</h2>
  <p>
    When I’m not making music or building projects, I’m usually travelling, cycling, swimming, or doing yoga and meditation.
    I try to keep life simple and healthy, and those habits shape my creative work — reminding me that technology can support
    wellbeing, presence, and connection (not just noise).
  </p>
