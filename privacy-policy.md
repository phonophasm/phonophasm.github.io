# Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Phonosphere (“the App”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to be used without creating an account and without providing personal information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any of the following:

- Personal identifying information (such as name, email address, or phone number)
- Account information
- Payment information
- Location data
- Contact lists
- User-generated text, voice, or messages

The App does not require you to sign in or create an account.

## 2. Information Processed by Meta (Platform Services)

When you use the App on a Meta Quest device, certain information may be processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. as part of their platform services.

This may include:

- Device and system information
- Platform-level usage data
- Store and entitlement checks

This information is handled according to Meta’s own Privacy Policy and is not accessible to us.

For more information, please refer to Meta’s Privacy Policy:  
https://www.meta.com/privacy/

## 3. App Functionality & On-Device Data

Any settings, preferences, or creative data created within the App (such as audio, visuals, or session data) are processed locally on your device.

We do not transmit this data to external servers.

Some data may be temporarily stored on your device to allow the App to function correctly. This data remains on the device unless you remove the App.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services.

If future updates introduce optional third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect any personal data from users of any age.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
