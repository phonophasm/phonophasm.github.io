PHONOSPHERE continues to evolve and grow.

Over the past weeks I’ve been refining the demo experience to make it more immersive, more intuitive, and more musical. This update focuses on spatial layout, usability, and giving players immediate creative tools.

## 🌍 A Full 360° Sphere of Sound

The biggest addition is a complete 360-degree sphere of sound.

Instead of interacting with isolated sound points, you’re now surrounded by a full sonic environment.  
This brings PHONOSPHERE closer to its core idea.

Music is not on a timeline.  
It exists in space.

## 🧭 Optimised Menu & Cleaner Interface

The interface has been simplified and refined.

- Clearer tool layout
- Streamlined instructions
- Reduced visual clutter
- Faster access to essential controls

The goal is to reduce friction — so you spend less time figuring out the interface and more time making sound.

PHONOSPHERE should feel playful, not technical.

![Inside the PHONOSPHERE demo user experience]({{ '/assets/posts/demo-update-1/inside.png' | relative_url }})

## 🎛 Two Demo Sample Sets

To help users jump straight into exploration, I’ve added two curated demo sample sets.

Each set has its own character and spatial behaviour, allowing you to:

- Explore rhythm and atmosphere
- Experiment with layering
- Feel how spatial placement changes perception

These sets act as starting points — frameworks for improvisation rather than fixed compositions.

## 🎨 A Cleaner Spatial Layout

The overall scene layout has been refined for clarity and balance.

Visual noise has been reduced, spacing has been adjusted, and interaction zones are more readable. The aim is to create an environment that feels intentional and calm — somewhere between instrument and meditation space.

## What’s Next?

This is still early access thinking. The roadmap ahead is exciting.

Upcoming development goals include:

### 🔊 True 3D Spatialised Sound

Moving beyond stereo placement toward deeper spatial audio integration, allowing sound to behave more physically in space.

### 🔁 Built-in Looper

Capture and layer performances in real time - building evolving soundscapes directly inside PHONOSPHERE.

### 🎚 Sampler Integration

Load your own sounds and reshape the instrument around your own sonic identity.

### 🌌 Optional Background Environments

Passthrough is powerful - but I also want to introduce alternative immersive backgrounds for different moods and performance contexts.

Forest. Ocean. Sky.  
Or something more abstract.

PHONOSPHERE is slowly becoming less of a demo and more of an instrument.

Thank you to everyone who has tested it, shared feedback, and explored the sphere.

More updates soon.
