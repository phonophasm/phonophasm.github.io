I'm very happy to share that **XPLONATI** is out today.

![XPLONATI album cover]({{ '/assets/images/xplonati/cover.png' | relative_url }})

XPLONATI is an album written using **PHONOSPHERE**, my VR instrument. It grew from the kind of musical exploration PHONOSPHERE is made for: moving through sound with my hands, following gestures and textures, and letting ideas develop inside an immersive space rather than beginning on a conventional timeline.

Alongside the VR performances, I added **isochronic tones** using a script I wrote for Reaper. That process brought another layer of pulse and movement into the record, connecting the physical, spatial way I compose in VR with a more detailed production workflow.

For me, this release is a snapshot of PHONOSPHERE as a creative instrument—not just something to demonstrate, but a place to actually write music. I hope XPLONATI offers a little space to listen closely, drift, and explore.
