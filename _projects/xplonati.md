# XPLONATI (2026)

*An album written with PHONOSPHERE, my VR instrument, and developed further with isochronic tones in Reaper.*

![XPLONATI album cover]({{ '/assets/images/xplonati/cover.png' | relative_url }})

## Overview

XPLONATI grew from composing inside PHONOSPHERE: moving through sounds with my hands, following texture and gesture, and allowing musical ideas to take shape in an immersive space rather than starting from a conventional timeline.

The album is a document of PHONOSPHERE as a working creative instrument. It is not only a technical experiment or a demonstration of the system, but a space for writing music.

## Process

Alongside the VR performances, I used a script written for Reaper to add isochronic tones. This introduced another layer of pulse and movement, connecting the physical and spatial process of composing in VR with a more detailed production workflow.

Together, those approaches shaped XPLONATI: an album built through exploration, spatial gesture, and close listening.
