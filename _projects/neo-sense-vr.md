# Neo-Sense — Interactive Sense Swapping in VR

*A research-driven VR project exploring how altered sensory mappings reshape perception, navigation, and embodied interaction.*

> **Status:** Completed research prototype. This page documents the project as a design and research exploration rather than a finished product.

![Hero](/assets/projects/Neo-Sense/hero.jpg "Neo-Sense VR — overview")

---

## Overview

**Neo-Sense** is a VR research project exploring **sense swapping** — the deliberate re-mapping of visual and auditory channels — to challenge habitual perception and reveal how users adapt to unfamiliar sensory conditions.

Built in Unity for the Oculus / Meta ecosystem, Neo-Sense rearranges where and how users receive sight and sound. By exchanging or relocating sensory cues, participants must **search, navigate, and interact** using unfamiliar perceptual strategies. The experience is intentionally playful and disorienting, encouraging reflection on **how space is learned through the senses** rather than taken for granted.

## What “sense swapping” means (plain English)

In everyday perception, vision is front-facing and hearing surrounds us. Neo-Sense intentionally **breaks this contract**.

Visual input can be split or redirected, while audio cues can be narrowed, shifted, or repositioned. Users then complete simple searching and navigation tasks, gradually discovering new scanning habits and strategies as they adapt to the altered sensory layout.

The focus is not confusion for its own sake, but **learning through adaptation**.

## Core interaction: Searching the space

Participants explore a colourful environment containing **eight simple, clearly differentiated objects** (numbers, shapes, and strong visual/audio cues).

Tasks are designed to maintain **low cognitive load** and high **sensory emphasis**. Instead of puzzle-solving, users rely on:

- following sound to locate a source  
- using split visual fields to scan space differently  
- coordinating head and body movement to resolve uncertainty  

The emphasis is on **embodied exploration** and **strategy-shifting**, not difficulty.

## Three sense-swap configurations

Neo-Sense includes three configurations to surface different adaptation behaviours:

### A — Ears Front, Eyes Left & Right  
Audio is focused straight ahead while visual input emphasises lateral views. Users often adopt wide side-to-side visual sweeps while homing in on a forward-facing audio cone.

### B — Ears Back, Eyes Left & Right  
Sound arrives from behind while vision remains lateral. This creates tension between backward auditory cues and sideways visual scanning, prompting unusual head-turn patterns.

### C — Ears Top, Eyes Front & Back  
Audio appears overhead while vision covers both forward and rear directions. This configuration disrupts strong forward-bias habits and reveals how quickly new scanning rituals emerge.

## Why this matters

Neo-Sense demonstrates that relatively small, deliberate changes to sensory routing can:

- shift navigation strategies  
- increase engagement and attentiveness  
- highlight perceptual plasticity  

Rather than treating perception as passive input, the project frames it as a **learned, adaptable skill**. Users actively renegotiate how they align sight, sound, and movement when familiar assumptions are removed.

---

## Design notes (condensed process)

- **Constraint-first design**  
  Tasks remain simple so sensory adaptation — not problem-solving — stays central.

- **Embodied UX**  
  Large cues, distinct objects, and readable spatial feedback are prioritised over complexity.

- **Iterative testing**  
  Early prototypes explored different combinations of visual splitting and audio relocation to balance surprise with learnability.

- **Observation-led refinement**  
  Users quickly developed new strategies, such as exaggerated head sweeps or reliance on “audio cones,” revealing emergent behaviours rather than prescribed ones.

## Tech stack

- **Engine:** Unity  
- **Device:** Oculus / Meta Quest (developed on Quest 2; principles extend to Quest 3)  
- **Implementation:** Custom camera routing and spatial audio mapping for split vision and relocated sound  
- **Environment:** Lightweight scenes designed for consistency and repeatable observation

---

## Applications & next steps

Neo-Sense points toward several possible directions:

- **Education:** Teaching spatial hearing and visual attention through lived experience  
- **Therapy & Training:** Safe environments for practising adaptation and spatial awareness  
- **Art & Interaction Design:** New grammars for exploratory games and immersive installations  

**Potential future work** includes more configurable swap modes, session logging and metrics, and tools for designers to author tasks and difficulty ramps.
