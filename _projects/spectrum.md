# Spectrum — Colours, Shapes & First Steps

*An early audio-visual project built from colour, instinct, and quiet experimentation.*

![Spectrum artwork sketches](/assets/images/spectrum.png)

Spectrum (2021) marks the starting point of *Phonophasm* as an audio-visual practice. Created during the slowed, uncertain rhythm of the Covid period, the project explores freeform songwriting alongside abstract drawing, allowing sound and image to evolve together without a fixed outcome in mind.

## Overview

Spectrum emerged at a moment when physical movement was limited and time felt diffuse. Rather than responding directly to external events, the project turned inward, using **colour and abstraction** as a way to explore internal states.

The approach was intentionally minimal:

- draw quickly, without rules or refinement,  
- let each drawing suggest a mood or direction,  
- translate that feeling into sound in the same sitting.

The result is a set of compact pieces tied more closely to **impressions and palettes** than to narrative or lyrics. Spectrum represents the first step toward treating **music and visuals as a single, intertwined process** rather than parallel outputs.

---

## Process

Key elements of how Spectrum was made:

- **Abstract sketching**  
  Loose, instinctive drawings acted as emotional anchors for each piece — prompts rather than plans.

- **Freeform recording**  
  Guitars, keys, textures, and noise were layered quickly, following feeling over structure.

- **Emotional snapshots**  
  Rather than explaining or narrating a moment in history, the project became an escape into colour — a quiet space for attention, curiosity, and release.

This workflow — visual prompt → sonic response — became the foundation for later *Phonophasm* projects.

---

## Sound & visuals

- Short tracks mapped loosely to specific colours (Purple, Pink, Blue, Cyan, etc.), each treated as a small self-contained world.
- Sound design focused on:
  - gentle motion,  
  - layered tones,  
  - drifting textures rather than conventional song forms.
- Visuals were kept deliberately simple and handmade, reinforcing the idea that **imperfect, playful creation** is a valid starting point.

## Why Spectrum matters

Spectrum is the **origin point** of the practice:

- The first deliberate attempt to bind drawing and sound into a single creative loop.
- The seed of ideas that later evolve into:
  - *Focal Point*,  
  - *Neo-Sense VR*,  
  - *Matsu VR*,  
  - *PHONOSPHERE* and other VR instruments.
- A reminder that much of the work that followed grew from:
  - spontaneity,  
  - abstraction as emotional language,  
  - and building immersive worlds from very simple beginnings.

Spectrum doesn’t aim to resolve ideas — it opens them.
